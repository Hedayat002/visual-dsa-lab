02 · Key idea

Even indices contribute +, odd indices contribute −. Put the largest squares in positive slots and the smallest squares in negative slots.
k = (n + 1) // 2
max score = sum(largest k squares) − sum(smallest n−k squares)

07 · Correctness takeaway
Every even index adds a square and every odd index subtracts one. Since rearrangement is allowed, the best assignment pairs the largest ⌈n/2⌉ squares with positive signs. Sorting by x² makes the two groups easy to select. Squaring means the original number's sign does not affect its contribution.
